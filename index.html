Virtual acoustics tour

Teatro Antico di Taormina - simulated acoustics

Simulated, not recorded: everything you hear of the theatre is the prediction of an uncalibrated computer model, played with a recorded sample (the default soprano is a dry, anechoic recording; the other samples are not anechoic and carry their own room sound or ambience); it is what our model predicts, not what it sounded like. Every view is a render of the study website's drawn model of the theatre, seen from the website's own seat: not a photograph, and not the geometry in which the sound was simulated (the direction toward the simulated voice position, seen from the picture's position and heard in the sound, differs by at most 2.5 degrees to the side and 3.3 degrees in height).

24 listening seats in each. Press Start Audio, then drag the picture to look around and pick other seats on the map. Best with headphones (a simple left/right stereo decode). Inside the tour, the switch button opens the other building at the same seat.
