THIRD-PARTY NOTICES for JSAmbisonics/ambisonics.umd.js (bundled with the CAVERN Template)

JSAmbisonics/ambisonics.umd.js is the browserify bundle of JSAmbisonics, published on npm as
"ambisonics" 0.4.0 (this file is byte-identical to that release's ambisonics.umd.js). Besides
JSAmbisonics by Archontis Politis and David Poirier-Quinot it contains the code of 22 other
packages (babel-runtime and core-js each in two versions) and two components inside numeric
with licences of their own. The CAVERN Template's LICENSE file (one folder up) does NOT cover any
of this code. Each part keeps its own licence; the full texts follow, section 1 being
JSAmbisonics' own licence (also shipped unchanged as JSAmbisonics/LICENSE).

The texts were collected on 2026-10-14 from each package's npm release or, where the release
carries no licence file, from its source repository at the matching tag or commit. They are
reproduced verbatim (leading and trailing blank lines removed).

Exact release not established (the bundle does not pin it; the candidates are named in the
section): bit-twiddle, gl-matrix, babel-runtime 6.x, babel-runtime 5.8.x, core-js 1.x and the
fractional-delay commit. Their licence texts are the same for every candidate, except the
copyright years of babel-runtime 6.x (section 24). get-float-time-domain-data declares the
MIT licence but publishes no licence text or copyright line (section 6).
